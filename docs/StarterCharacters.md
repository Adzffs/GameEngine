# Embervale starter cast

This is the character contract for roadmap milestone J1. Every friendly character needs a recognizable silhouette, a village role, a personal want or problem, a distinct speaking style, a relationship to another character, a mechanical purpose, and optional examine text.

| Character | Silhouette / colour | Village role | Want or problem | Voice | Relationship | Mechanical purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mara Vale | Blue warden coat, gold sash | Warden and quartermaster | Prepare newcomers before the Cinder Warden reaches Embervale | Practical, warm, short field lessons | Trusts Torren but worries about his pride | Tutorial, starter quests, supplies shop |
| Torren Ash | Broad leather apron, ember-red gloves | Smith and workshop keeper | Prove his new steelwork can protect the village | Blunt, competitive, speaks through craft metaphors | Respects Mara; rivals Brann over material quality | Forging guidance and crafting-dilemma quest |
| Elia Reed | Tall green coat, brass key ring | Banker and record keeper | Recover a missing village ledger without causing panic | Precise, dry humour, remembers every debt | Quietly helps Nessa fund woodland restoration | Banking guidance, recovery explanation, investigation lead |
| Nessa Thorne | Leaf-green hood, crooked walking staff | Forester and herbalist | Stop the woodland becoming hostile without stripping it bare | Patient, observant, asks questions before giving answers | Distrusts Torren's appetite for timber; close to Elia | Gathering guidance, tonic context, woodland quest branch |
| Brann Deepwell | Compact stone-grey coat, copper lamp | Miner and surveyor | Reopen the old route while hiding how frightened he is of Stonebacks | Boastful stories that collapse into honest detail | Competes with Torren; protective of Sera | Mining progression, route clues, material choice |
| Sera Flint | Rust-red cloak, narrow bow silhouette | Hunter and road scout | Track why Ashfangs are leaving their normal territory | Sparse, direct, notices details others miss | Brann treats her like family; reports to Mara | Combat preparation, threat escalation, elite encounter lead |

## Examine lines

- Mara: “Mara watches the road and quietly counts every traveller who returns.”
- Torren: “Every tool in Torren’s reach has been repaired twice and praised three times.”
- Elia: “Elia’s key ring is organised more carefully than most people’s lives.”
- Nessa: “Leaves and tiny labelled bottles fill every pocket of Nessa’s coat.”
- Brann: “Brann’s lamp is polished; his boots suggest the mine was less cooperative.”
- Sera: “Sera studies the treeline as if it has just said something important.”

## Relationship loop

Mara needs Torren’s equipment; Torren needs Brann’s ore and Nessa’s timber; Brann relies on Sera to scout dangerous routes; Sera reports changing threats to Mara; Nessa relies on Elia to protect restoration supplies; Elia needs the player to recover evidence that connects the whole village mystery.

This loop should surface through optional dialogue and quest choices, not only quest exposition.
