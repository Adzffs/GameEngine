# Embervale starter cast

**Design contract:** Character roles, voices, wants, relationships and examine lines are design intent; individual dialogue implementation is tracked separately. See the [content catalogue](embervale/05-game/CONTENT_CATALOGUE.md) and [game design](embervale/05-game/GAME_DESIGN.md). The earlier J1 label is historical; the current [master roadmap](embervale/02-roadmap/MASTER_ROADMAP.md) uses R0–R10.

This is the starter character design contract. Every friendly character needs a recognizable silhouette, a village role, a personal want or problem, a distinct speaking style, a relationship to another character, a mechanical purpose, and optional examine text.

## Examine lines

- Mara: “Mara watches the road and quietly counts every traveller who returns.”
- Torren: “Every tool in Torren’s reach has been repaired twice and praised three times.”
- Elia: “Elia’s key ring is organised more carefully than most people’s lives.”
- Nessa: “Leaves and tiny labelled bottles fill every pocket of Nessa’s coat.”
- Brann: “Brann’s lamp is polished; his boots suggest the mine was less cooperative.”
- Sera: “Sera studies the treeline as if it has just said something important.”

## Relationship loop

Mara needs Torren’s equipment; Torren needs Brann’s ore and Nessa’s timber; Brann relies on Sera to scout dangerous routes; Sera reports changing threats to Mara; Nessa relies on Elia to protect restoration supplies; Elia needs the player to recover evidence that connects the whole village mystery.

This loop should surface through optional dialogue and quest choices, not only quest exposition.


## Current quest continuity

The elite is **Cinder Warden**. Mara's Tracks in the Ash sends the player to
Sera and back; its completion opens Torren's Shield or Shelter. Torren and Nessa
offer coin/food alternatives for recorded crafting, while the player keeps the
shield. Sera then offers Fangs at the Gate. Heart of the Ash ends with a victory
report to Mara for coins or Brann for Steel bars. Either contact completes it;
there is no trophy inventory requirement.

Current conversations explain acceptance before progress, actual station/bank
use, preparation and the next quest. Elia's ledger investigation remains future
character intent. The cast's larger personal goals are not additional journal
quests. [Current mechanics, rewards, guidance and validation](embervale/06-development/QUEST_CONTINUITY_2026-11-19.md).
